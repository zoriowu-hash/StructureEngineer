# Insulation (isolasjon)

Insulation is the layer in walls and roofs that keeps heat inside the house. To work well it has to stay dry and still, so it is protected by membranes on each side.

## Wind Barrier (for Walls and Roofs)

A **wind barrier** is a type of membrane: a specialized, breathable protective layer installed on the outer (cold) side of the insulation in walls and roofs.

### Purpose

- Stops cold outside air from blowing into the insulation layers, which would otherwise cause heat loss.
- Breathable (vapour-open): lets moisture escape outward from the structure so the insulation stays dry.
