# StructureEngineer

Personal study notes on building structures: structural components, design knowledge, and terminology (Norwegian / English / Chinese), with diagrams and links to reference material.

Notes live under `notes/`, one folder per topic.

## Notes by topic

### Roof

- [Roof Basics](notes/roof/roof-basics.md): general roof knowledge, roof types, parts and attic terms (Norwegian / English / Chinese)
- [Rafter Roof](notes/roof/rafter-roof.md): rafter roof components, terms, and design references
- [Purlin Roof](notes/roof/purlin-roof.md): purlin roof components and design references
- [Roof Battens](notes/roof/roof-battens.md): roof layers from rafter to tiles, counter-battens and tile battens

### Insulation

- [Insulation](notes/insulation/insulation.md): insulation for walls and roofs, including the wind barrier membrane

### Norwegian Book

- [Norwegian Book Notes](notes/norwegian-book/README.md): chapter/section notes from the Norwegian book *Etterisolering av småhus* (retrofit insulation of small houses): page pictures, Norwegian text, English translation, and Q&A
